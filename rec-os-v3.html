90-Second State Shift +
  1. Stand, loosen your shoulders, and reset your posture.
  2. Use a comfortable slow-breathing rhythm for a few cycles.
  3. Ask: “What makes the next 25 minutes a win?”
  4. Write the first small step. Begin.
Box Breathing (4-4-4-4) +

Inhale for four counts, hold for four, exhale for four and pause for four if comfortable. Repeat gently. Stop if you feel dizzy or unwell.
