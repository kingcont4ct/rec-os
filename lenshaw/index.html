What we do

Services tailored to your organization

From day-to-day bookkeeping to full HR support, we offer the financial backbone that keeps churches, non-profits and small businesses running smoothly.

Bookkeeping

Accurate, timely books so leadership always has a clear picture of the organization's financial health. We reconcile accounts, track contributions and prepare clean, audit-ready reports.

Payroll

Full-service payroll processing for staff and clergy — including tax filings, housing allowances and reporting — handled accurately and on schedule.

HR & Compliance

Employee handbooks, document creation and ongoing HR compliance support so your organization stays aligned with current employment law.

Financial Consulting

Strategic guidance on accounting entries, payroll questions and operational finance — available in person or by video call when you need expert input.

Training

Hands-on training for your staff and volunteers in best accounting practices, helping your in-house team build lasting skills and confidence.

Process Setup

Custom workflows, forms and procedures designed around how your organization actually operates — so good habits become routine.
