FUEL • FORM • FLOW • FORTIFY

Red Zone Health Manual

A practical starting guide. Use the four pillars to build and review sustainable routines—not to guarantee performance or health outcomes.

01 / Fuel — Nutrition & hydration

Build meals around varied vegetables, fruit, protein sources, fibre-rich foods and hydration. Use the planner to make decisions before busy days.

Try: Plan three reliable meals. Prepare one ingredient batch. Keep water available.

Open Meal Planner →

02 / Form — Movement

Choose movement you can repeat safely. General adult guidelines suggest 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity each week and muscle-strengthening activity on at least two days, adjusted to ability and individual circumstances.

Try: Take a short walk or mobility break. Schedule your next session. Track what you did instead of what you intended.

03 / Flow — Attention & recovery rituals

Set a specific outcome, reduce distractions, and work for a defined time block. Use your own observations to decide whether a ritual helps your concentration.

Try: 3-minute preparation, one priority, 25-minute focus block, one sentence of reflection.

Start the Ramp Timer →

04 / Fortify — Rest & resilience

Protect opportunities for sleep, recovery and rest. Consistent routines help you notice what actually supports your energy. Most adults generally require 7–9 hours of sleep, though individual needs vary.

Try: Record sleep and waking energy in your daily journal. Review patterns weekly.

Run the experiment

Choose one small action, repeat it for seven days, record what happened, and adjust based on evidence. The Red Zone is an individual practice rather than a guaranteed state.
